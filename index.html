How the system works

  1. Collect. Ad platforms, web analytics, CRM and billing feed one dataset.
  2. Stitch identities. UTM tags, cookies and email addresses link anonymous visits to a named lead.
  3. Rebuild journeys. Every touchpoint is ordered in time, from first exposure to payment.
  4. Attribute revenue. Five models share each deal's value across its touchpoints. The data-driven model measures how much conversion drops when a channel is removed (Markov chain).
  5. Detect leaks. Stage conversion is compared with benchmarks, and each channel's share of leads is compared with its share of revenue.
  6. Recommend. The largest recoverable gaps are ranked in rupees.
Data on this page is simulated (8,000 leads, fixed random seed) to demonstrate the method. To use real data, replace the makeJourneys() function in this file with rows loaded from your CRM export.
